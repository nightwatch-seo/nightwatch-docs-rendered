Is there a limit number of times I can crawl the Site Audit?

Written by Maja Nagelj
March 22, 2024

With Nightwatch, you're free to conduct as many site audits as you need—there's no cap on the number of audits you can run. The only limitation pertains to the number of pages you can audit, which varies based on your subscription plan.
